About Company

BrandDESING has come into existence after many years of experience to make Your brand . It deals with digital large format printing, cutting graphics and gluing, based in Brno.

We have long been collaborating with companies in the production of graphics for trade fairs, exhibitions and congresses, not only in the Czech Republic but also around the world . We carry out car stickers, shop windows and the like. Production of 3D log and textile printing. Production of advertising is ensured from design, through production to sticking itself.
